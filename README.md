## 👨‍💻 Engineering Philosophy & About Me

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at **IIIT Nagpur** (Specialization in **Data Science**), passionate about designing high-performance, intelligent, and scalable distributed systems. 

My engineering journey revolves around bridging the gap between theoretical algorithms, modern generative AI pipelines, and rock-solid backend infrastructure:

- 🧠 **AI & LLM Orchestration:** Architecting resilient **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** pipelines, hybrid vector search, chunking & re-ranking strategies, and multi-model agentic workflows.
- ⚡ **Backend & Distributed Systems:** Building low-latency asynchronous microservices using **FastAPI**, **Node.js**, **Redis caching**, and containerized multi-tier deployments.
- 🧩 **Algorithmic Problem Solving:** Solved **370+ problems on LeetCode** with a contest rating of **~1700**, mastering core Data Structures, Dynamic Programming, and Graph Algorithms.
- 🌐 **Open-Source Impact:** **900+ open-source contributions** and **30+ public repositories**, including upstream contributions to industry staples like **Axios**, **Fastify**, **Hugging Face Transformers**, and **Appsmith**.
